Privacy Policy for HTML to APK

Effective date: November 10, 2026
Developer: Elijah Wougamon
App: HTML to APK (package com.livewire.converter2)

Data we collect

None. HTML to APK does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or any other user data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no internet access of its own.

What stays on your phone

Everything the app works with stays on your device:

  • The HTML files you pick are read only to build your app. They are not uploaded anywhere.
  • The apps you build are saved on your phone, and installed from your phone.
  • Each phone generates its own signing key the first time you build an app. The key is stored in the Android KeyStore on that phone and never leaves the device.

The apps you build

The apps you create with HTML to APK are your own apps. Their content and behavior are determined by the HTML file you supply. This policy covers the HTML to APK converter itself, not the apps you build with it.

Third parties

HTML to APK does not share data with any third party, because it collects no data to share.

Children

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the app's Google Play store listing.
